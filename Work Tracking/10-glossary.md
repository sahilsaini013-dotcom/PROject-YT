# Glossary

## AI Review Inbox

Trainer-facing queue of AI-generated client recommendations.

## Client Readiness

A summary of whether a client appears ready to train harder, maintain, or recover based on training, nutrition, recovery, and adherence.

## Coaching Loop

The core cycle: trainer assigns work, client completes/logs it, Training Hub analyzes it, trainer reviews and adjusts.

## Nutrition Hub

Product area for nutrition targets, meal logs, water, supplements, meal plans, and trainer review.

## Recovery Hub

Product area for sleep, soreness, mood, energy, stress, pain, and wearable summaries.

## Trainer-Controlled AI

AI that suggests actions to the trainer but does not automatically make sensitive coaching decisions.
