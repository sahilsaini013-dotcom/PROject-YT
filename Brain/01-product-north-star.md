# Product North Star

## Product Name

Training Hub

## One-Line Promise

Training Hub helps personal trainers run smarter coaching businesses by combining training, nutrition, recovery, client communication, and AI-assisted decision support in one platform.

## Core Idea

Most fitness apps track workouts. Training Hub should help trainers understand the full client picture:

- Are they training consistently?
- Are they recovering well?
- Are they eating enough to support the goal?
- Are they emotionally and mentally checked in?
- Are they ready to be pushed harder, or should the trainer pull back?

The app should not just collect data. It should turn client behavior into clear coaching actions.

## Product Pillars

1. Training
2. Nutrition
3. Recovery
4. Progress
5. Communication
6. AI trainer assistance
7. Business tools

## Primary Audience

Personal trainers who manage multiple clients and need a cleaner system for programming, accountability, progress review, and coaching decisions.

## Secondary Audience

Clients who want a guided experience that feels personal, structured, and connected to their trainer.

## What Makes Training Hub Different

Training Hub is not just a workout tracker and not just a meal logger. It connects training, food, recovery, and adherence so the trainer can make better decisions.

The app's most important question is:

> What does this client need from their trainer right now?
