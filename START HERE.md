# START HERE

Welcome to the Training Hub project folder.

This folder has three different kinds of project material. They are supposed to do different jobs.

## 1. Training Hub Plan.md

Use this when you want the big-picture product plan.

It explains:

- What Training Hub is
- Who it is for
- The major product pillars
- The planned architecture
- The build phases

## 2. Brain

Use this when you want the project's memory.

The Brain folder answers:

- What do we believe about the product?
- Who are the users?
- How should the AI coach think?
- What are the key user flows?
- What data does the app need?
- What questions are still open?

Think of Brain as the project's source of truth.

## 3. Work Tracking

Use this when you want to manage the work.

The Work Tracking folder answers:

- What are we doing now?
- What is in the backlog?
- What is in the current sprint?
- What decisions have we made?
- What risks are open?
- What shipped?
- What should we report this week?

Think of Work Tracking as the project's operating board.

## Quick Rule

- If it defines the product, put it in `Brain`.
- If it tracks tasks, status, risks, releases, or weekly work, put it in `Work Tracking`.
- If it summarizes the whole idea, update `Training Hub Plan.md`.
